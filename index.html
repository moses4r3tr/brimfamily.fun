Every hour

Filled by every trade. Poured every hour.

Each token runs the same cycle on its own. There is nothing to switch on and nothing to claim.

    CollectA share of every transfer is held back, on the curve or in the pool.

    SwapOnce an hour it is sold for the pair coin: SOL on a SOL pair.
  3. 03
    CountEvery wallet holding $5 or more of the token is counted.
  4. 04
    SendHolders by balance, the creator's share, and the burn.
  5. 05
    RecordEach transfer is listed on the token's page with its signature.

The flywheel

Every trade turns the wheel.

Brim's share of each trade goes into its treasury, and the treasury goes back into Brim.

  1. TradesEvery trade on a Brim token pays a small share to Brim.
  2. TreasuryThat share collects in Brim's treasury, on-chain for anyone to see.
  3. GrowthThe treasury pays for what makes launches work: the hourly payouts, better tools and getting new tokens seen.
  4. More launchesMore launches bring more trades, and the wheel turns again.

Questions

Short answers

When do holders get paid?

Every hour. If an hour's tax is too small to be worth a swap, it waits and is added to the next hour's.

What are payouts made in?

The coin the token trades against: SOL on a SOL pair, and the pair's own coin on any other.

Is there anything to claim?

No. The holders' share, the creator's share and the burn are all sent automatically.

Who isn't paid?

Wallets holding less than $5 of the token when the hour is counted, and the curve and pool accounts themselves.

What happens at graduation?

When the curve fills, the token moves into a Raydium pool with its liquidity locked for good, and the hourly payouts carry on wherever it trades.

Does Brim vet tokens?

No. Anyone can launch, and a token can lose all of its value. Brim runs the launch and the payouts; it doesn't review or endorse what gets launched.
